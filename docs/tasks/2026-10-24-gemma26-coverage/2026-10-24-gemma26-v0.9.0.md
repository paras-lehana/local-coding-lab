## Why test it

The installed official QAT artifact is smaller than the higher Qwen quant and
was already retained as a fallback. Google's quantization-aware training is a
different recipe from simply increasing a post-training quant's bit count.
It warrants its missing modern coding/file-workflow checks, not an assumed
quality upgrade. The old private19/36 result in about400seconds remains
historical; its instruction omissions prevent calling it the quality leader.
