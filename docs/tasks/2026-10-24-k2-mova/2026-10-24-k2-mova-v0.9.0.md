## Why this candidate

The publisher describes sparse feed-forward and mixture-of-values attention,
native high reasoning and524288-token context. Its official serving example
uses two H200 GPUs; those claims do not establish performance on one10GB3080.
This trial tests the unresolved candidate using the same instruction/file
workflows and20-minute whole-prompt limit as the other local models.
[Official model card](https://huggingface.co/IFM/K2-Horizon-MoVA-36B-A4B).

This architecture's estimated64K KV is3.375GiB inq4 versus6.375GiB inq8.
The header estimates18.851GiB host expert tensors,0.336GiB embeddings and
1.634GiB other GPUweights. Stored spans are not measured allocations. The
attention-value override is essential: `--n-cpu-moe` alone does not cover
those tensors. Its regex was checked against the actual tensor naming before
load; the real loader allocation still needs verification.

Prefer `k2-mova-q4-cpu48-vcpu-kvq4-64k` if its fresh18GiB RAM gate passes.
Otherwise the separate `k2-mova-q4-resident8-calibration` needs14.5GiB RAM,
10GiB availablecommit and6200MiB deviceVRAM, with fixed8GiB residency and
6.5GiB reserve. Both retain stop floors2.5GiB RAM/1GiB commit/400MiB VRAM
and84C. This is a paging experiment, not proof that the model is fully resident.
The launcher's complete hash check and bounded smoke precede quality work.
