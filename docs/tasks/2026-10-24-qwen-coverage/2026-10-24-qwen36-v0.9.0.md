## Why these candidates

Qwen3.6-35B-A3B Q4 is the retained instruction-quality reference. Its smaller
IQ3_S and IQ4_XS variants may fit the owner's open applications with less
paging. The goal is completed, source-grounded coding inside1200seconds,
not maximum GPU occupancy or a nominal quantization label. Earlier Q5/Q6
audit results did not establish a quality gain; Q6 exceeded the current limit.
The historical larger file is specifically UD-Q6_K_XL (31,843,777,504bytes).
Plain UD-Q6_K (29,308,320,736bytes) is a distinct untested artifact. Current
publisher metadata still matches the pinned repository revision; a general
guide update is not evidence of a new revision to the tested weights.
